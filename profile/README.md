## 🌟 About GOMUGOMU

Welcome to the official **GOMUGOMU** organization on GitHub. We are a **technology company** where engineering and creativity come together to build products people actually enjoy using.

* 🐾 **What We're Building:** **Wufftail**, a complete software ecosystem for pet stores and pet care services
* 🧩 **Our Approach:** Well-engineered systems, thoughtful design and products built to work together
* 👥 **Our Team:** Engineers who care about the craft, from backend systems to the final pixel
* 🔒 **Our Repositories:** Private, and accessible to GOMUGOMU team members
* 👀 **What Drives Us:** Solving real problems with elegant code AND beautiful design
